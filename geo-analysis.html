Key patterns from the day/night split:
1. TINY LOCAL incidents have almost no day/night difference (534-605m night vs 495-575m day). Partner can't find the fault regardless of time — location intelligence helps equally.
2. MAJOR incidents show 1.4-1.7x night penalty — partner knows something's wrong but doesn't act at night. Worst: AREA+SMALL+MAJOR goes from 437m (day) to 737m (night) = 1.7x.
3. MEDIUM/LARGE LOCAL incidents barely have a night penalty (62→72m, 72→107m) — these are obvious enough that partners fix them regardless of time.
4. Day P80 is often WORSE than night P80 for TINY incidents (1,128m vs 765m) — the long tail during the day is worse because daytime TINY incidents that don't self-resolve get stuck behind the partner's higher-priority daytime work.

When a customer raises a ticket during a TINY outage, P50 TAT jumps from 535m to 1,312m (2.5x worse). Ticket = marker of the WORST outages, not a resolution accelerator. These are the ones where the partner has zero clue and the customer is suffering the most. Ticket overlay flips priority: ticket + outage = real pain, must act NOW.
Ticket also negates false causes. If a customer is calling CS saying "internet down" → the device isn't powered off by the customer, the router isn't switched off, and it's not an expired plan issue. Ticket = the outage is real and the customer is actively suffering. This eliminates the power-off/device-off hypothesis for that incident.

The Framework
Outage Classification Tags (from Data)
Every outage gets tagged along 3 axes. The combination determines the probable cause, action, and what shows on the map.
Tag: SPLITTER_FAULT

Geo: TIGHT (<500m) + Concurrent: <5 + Size: TINY/SMALL

~5,800 incidents/week. P50: 592m.
Map pin at device centroid. "Technician bhejein is location pe."

+TICKET? → P50 jumps to 1,280m. Priority: CRITICAL.

Tag: OLT_POP_FAULT

Concurrent: 10+ OR Geo: WIDE + Size: MEDIUM/LARGE

~41,000 incidents/week (mostly as many TINYs). P50: 64-109m (when obvious) to 607m (when fragmented as TINYs).
"Check OLT/power at your POP."

+TICKET? → Confirms it's not power-off. Real infrastructure failure.

Tag: ISP_BACKHAUL

WIDE + SMALL/MEDIUM + MAJOR severity + multiple partners same area

~400 incidents/week. P50: 497m.
"ISP issue. We're escalating. You can't fix this."

+TICKET? → P50 = 470m. Customer suffering but nobody can act locally.

Tag: CUSTOMER_DEVICE

SINGLE device + No neighbors down + No concurrent

~47,000 incidents/week (LOCAL) + 63K (MICRO).
MICRO: P50 67m (self-resolves). LOCAL: P50 537m.

+TICKET? → Not power-off. Real device or auth issue. "Router restart karein."

Tag: TRUNK_FIBER

Geo: AREA (500m-3km) + Concurrent: 3-10 + Size: SMALL/MEDIUM

~500 incidents/week. P50: 135-177m.
"Main fiber trunk between POP and area splitters. Check trunk route."

Tag: POWER_LIKELY

WIDE + Concurrent 10+ + NO ticket from any customer

Subset of OLT_POP_FAULT. When 10+ concurrent but zero customers call → likely area power outage.
"DG set check karein ya bijli ka wait karein."

Ticket negates this tag. If even 1 customer calls → not power, it's real infra.

What the Partner Sees
Per-Outage Map Link
Each outage alert includes a link to a map view. Not a dashboard — a single-incident view with diagnosis.

Map View Structure

  • Center: Centroid of affected device lat/lngs
  • Red pins: Affected devices (with customer name/address on tap)
  • Amber pins: Customers who raised service tickets (the pain signal)
  • Green pins: Nearby HEALTHY devices (shows the fault boundary — "it's down HERE but fine 200m away")
  • Purple diamond: Nearest splitter location (if mapped)
  • Info card overlay: Classification tag, probable cause, action, "what NOT to do", Google Maps navigation link to fault centroid
  • Ticket badge: If customer(s) raised tickets → badge showing "2 customers already called CS" with ticket time

When Partner Has Multiple Active Outages

The WhatsApp message links to a partner-level overview map instead of single-incident:

  • All active outages as clusters, color-coded by tag (red=splitter, amber=OLT, purple=ISP, grey=device)
  • Bottom summary: "2 splitter faults to fix (route link) | 1 OLT issue (check POP) | 8 device-level (auto-SMS sent)"
  • Tap any cluster → drills into single-incident map
